# Drawing / animation review against the script

## A. Character logic (all confirmed wrong)
1. Hanging Tom holds a rifle — a bound prisoner has no weapon. Needs a dedicated upside-down
   figure: ankles roped, arms dangling toward the ground, scarf hanging down, no gun.
2. Infiltration team: only Ryley had a suit. Both must be suited — Tom keeps the yellow scarf
   over the suit so he stays findable.
3. Safe-room guard holds a rifle while playing games. Should hold only the console, head down.
4. The irradiated guards hold rifles. They have lost their minds — arms limp / drifting, no weapons.
5. The killed guard: no evidence of the fight. Needs a dropped rifle and a small red mark.

## B. Continuity (the bigger miss)
6. Scene 2: the jet's missile just floats in the sky. In the original it hits the helicopter.
   Every missile should travel its own drawn arc and end where the blast is.
   - S2 silo missile  -> lettuce base blast
   - S2 jet missile   -> helicopter blast
   - S3 lettuce missile -> tomato roof blast
   - S4 jet missile   -> risen platform blast
   - S4 tomato bomb   -> centre blast
7. One figure template for everyone. Poses must read the role: sniper aims down, falling man
   flails, rope man grips with one hand, gamer looks down, dead man lies down.

## C. Explosions
8. A regular 12-point star is a symbol, not an explosion. Rebuild as: asymmetric rays +
   bumpy fireball blobs (3 layers) + white core + shockwave ring + flying debris.
9. The Tomato Bomb should not be the same shape as a missile hit — it is a superweapon.
   Give it its own mushroom cloud: stem, bumpy cap, base roll, flash core.

## D. Animation
10. Missiles should fly their arcs (offset-path), not sit still.
11. Blasts need a shockwave ring that expands, plus debris that flies outward.
12. Rotor/prop already spinning; add jet exhaust flicker, fire flicker, radiation twinkle.
