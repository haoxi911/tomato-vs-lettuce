The Tomato Kingdom War

The Lettuce Kingdom attacks first. Both sides have a missile silo, and both sides fight in the sky. But the real story is about a soldier on the ground named Tom — and what is buried under the Lettuce Kingdom.

Who to watch for

Tom

Yellow scarf, in all five pictures. He keeps the scarf on even over a radiation suit.

The Tomato King

Gold crown, red robe. He is the one who gives the order for the Tomato Bomb.

Ryley, the SWAT diver

Yellow radiation suit and air tank. He came back alive from planet 4546B.

Scene One · The Attack Begins

Two Kingdoms, One Sky

The Lettuce Kingdom strikes first, so the Tomato Kingdom sends up its best supersonic jet. The Lettuce Kingdom answers with a helicopter. High above everything, a Tomato drone carries a sniper up and lets him go. There he puts a hole straight through an enemy paratrooper's chute. That soldier is not landing. Below them a Lettuce soldier swings on a rope tied to the helicopter's tail, trading fire with the machine gun on the Tomato roof. And down on the ground, one man keeps shooting at the enemy wall: Tom.

Find Tom: bottom middle, yellow scarf, firing right.

Scene Two · Direct Hit

First Missile

The Tomato silo fires. The missile flies the whole way across the field and knocks the Lettuce base over. In the same moment the supersonic jet lets go of its own missile — watch it fly down the line and hit the helicopter. The Lettuce Kingdom still has one card left: their drone drops a soldier straight at the Tomato roof to break the machine gun. And Tom? Tom has not moved. He is still shooting at the wall.

Find Tom: still on the ground, still shooting right. He has not moved an inch.

Scene Three · They Hit Back

The Counterstrike

Now the Lettuce silo fires. Their missile comes screaming back the other way and lands right on the Tomato roof — the machine gun and every weapon up there are smashed to pieces. The Tomato Kingdom does not stop. Paratroopers drop onto the enemy roof to take out the laser guns that are still firing; one of them is already dead metal. And on the ground, Tom keeps doing the exact same thing: shooting at the base. Not at people. Just the base.

Find Tom: same spot, same aim. Nobody has figured out why yet.

Scene Four · Two Betrayals

The Tomato Bomb

The Lettuce base is wrecked — and then the ground opens and a whole new set of high-tech weapons rises up out of it, including a second rocket launcher. A Tomato jet fires, and the missile goes straight into the new machines.

Then somebody is hanging upside down at the edge of the picture, roped by the ankles, and it is Tom. He was a Lettuce Kingdom spy the whole time. Years of it. That is why he only ever shot at walls — he was faking every attack. A spy in the family and a nuclear weapon buried next door: the King has heard enough. He sends up the Tomato Kingdom's strongest weapon. The Tomato Bomb comes down between the two kingdoms and goes off like a small sun, and Tom hangs there and shakes.

Find Tom: hanging upside down on the left, scarf pointing at the ground.

Between Scene Four and Scene Five

Tom's Choice

Hanging there upside down, Tom starts remembering. He came to the Tomato Kingdom when he was very small. He met his best friends here. He got married here. His kids have never lived anywhere else. He was born in the Lettuce Kingdom — but this is the place he actually loves.

And he knows something nobody else does: the nuclear weapon under the Lettuce base is already armed. In two days it goes off, and his home and the whole Tomato Kingdom go with it. He is done watching his friends die for the wrong side of the story.

So Tom switches sides for real, and offers to lead the way in.

Scene Five · Two Men Go Down

Shut It Down

Tom teams up with Ryley, the best SWAT operator the Tomato Kingdom has — the one who made it home from planet 4546B and knows how to move in a radiation suit. Both of them suit up. One knows the enemy, the other knows how to survive down there. They rope down the launch silo together.

What they find on level B1 is strange. The guards left behind have all gone wrong from the radiation: one is walking upside down on the ceiling, one is floating in the air with his arms out, and one locked himself in the shielded safe room — no suit, no way out, so he just sits in there playing video games forever. None of them are holding a weapon any more. Only one guard is still together enough to stop them, and he does not stop them for long.

Down on B2 they find the launcher. They do not have the password, so they do it the rough way, and the whole reactor circuit blows out and catches fire. Good thing there is a CO₂ bottle on the wall. They put the fire out — and then point the same bottle at the floor, turn it into a jet, and ride it up the air duct back to the surface.

Find Tom: twice. On B1 roping in, and again on B2 by the fire extinguisher — yellow suit, red scarf-belt, scarf over the shoulder.

Tomato Kingdom Wins

Every Lettuce Kingdom base near the Tomato Kingdom is destroyed. Their soldiers are gone. The nuclear launcher is dead, the fire is out, and two men rode a fire extinguisher home through an air duct. One of them was supposed to be the enemy.
